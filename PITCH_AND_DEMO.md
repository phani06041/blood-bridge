# Blood Bridge: pitch + demo video

## 60-second pitch (top-5 finals)
**Hook (0–10s):** "Raise your hand if you've seen an 'URGENT!! O-negative blood needed' message on WhatsApp. Did anyone ever tell you it was fulfilled, or whether it was even real?"
**Problem (10–25s):** "India is short of about a million units of blood a year. Families forward unverified messages that never get updated or closed, and scammers now use them to ask for 'processing fees'."
**Demo (25–50s):** "Blood Bridge is a Fetch.ai agent inside ASI:One. I type 'Need 1 unit O-negative at Manipal Hospital, contact Ravi'. It verifies the request first, lists the nearest blood banks with phone numbers and live government stock, matches donors who are compatible, within 15 km and eligible, writes one clean WhatsApp message with a request code, and when a donor taps 'I can donate', the family sees them, and closing the request stops the forwards."
**Close (50–60s):** "Built today on Agentverse, ASI-1 as its only brain. Blood Bridge: verified requests, the nearest blood, and a loop that actually closes."
